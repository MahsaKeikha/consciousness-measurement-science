# Theory Landscape and Discriminating Measurements

The purpose of theory review here is not to produce a popularity ranking. It is to extract unique measurement predictions.

## Global Neuronal Workspace Theory

Broad idea: conscious information gains widespread availability through ignition/broadcast.

Measurement candidates:
- long-range communication;
- late widespread activation;
- access/report consequences;
- perturbation of workspace nodes.

Key risk: report, decision, and post-perceptual processing can mimic workspace signatures.

## Integrated Information Theory

Broad idea: consciousness corresponds to irreducible causal/informational structure.

Measurement candidates:
- causal integration/differentiation;
- posterior cortical content structure;
- perturbational complexity as a practical relative, not direct full-phi calculation.

Key risk: computational/axiomatic commitments and mapping from formal quantity to experience remain contested.

## Recurrent Processing

Broad idea: recurrent sensory processing is central to conscious content.

Measurement candidates:
- feedback timing;
- recurrent laminar interactions;
- masking and interruption paradigms.

Key risk: recurrent processing may occur without reportable awareness and may require additional conditions.

## Higher-Order Accounts

Broad idea: a mental state is conscious when represented at a higher order.

Measurement candidates:
- metacognitive sensitivity;
- confidence/report dissociations;
- prefrontal/higher-order representation signatures.

Key risk: separating higher-order awareness from report and executive demand.

## Predictive/active-inference families

Broad idea: conscious content may depend on hierarchical inference and precision-weighted prediction.

Measurement candidates:
- prediction-error dynamics;
- precision manipulation;
- hierarchical representational geometry.

Key risk: predictive processing is broad and may explain cognition without uniquely explaining consciousness.

## Dynamical / complexity accounts

Broad idea: conscious states occupy rich, metastable, integrated-yet-differentiated dynamical regimes.

Measurement candidates:
- entropy and Lempel-Ziv complexity;
- effective connectivity;
- criticality/metastability;
- perturbational complexity.

Key risk: complexity is not unique to consciousness and can be high in systems with no established experience.

## Structural approaches

Broad idea: scientific progress may come from mapping the formal relational structure of experience to physical structure.

Measurement candidates:
- phenomenal-neural geometry alignment;
- topology and invariants;
- structure-preserving mappings.

Key risk: structural correspondence may underdetermine ontology and may not explain why the structure feels like anything.

## Fundamental / dual-aspect / panpsychist families

Broad idea: experiential properties are fundamental or paired with physical/informational structure.

Scientific requirement:
- a bridge principle precise enough to generate unique empirical predictions;
- prediction beyond standard neural models;
- falsification conditions.

Without unique measurement predictions, such views remain metaphysical possibilities rather than operational measurement theories.

## Quantum-specific theories

Scientific requirement:
- identify a quantum variable that is necessary/specific at relevant biological scales;
- predict a consciousness-linked effect not reproduced by classical open-system dynamics;
- show perturbational control and replication.

Quantum terminology is not evidence by itself.

## Adversarial methodology

The 2025 large-scale adversarial test of IIT and GNWT showed why theory comparison must be preregistered. Both theories received partial support and substantial challenges.

This project will adopt the same principle: the best experiment is one where plausible theories predict different outcomes in advance.
