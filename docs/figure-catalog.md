## Figure discipline

A Research III visual is not evidence by itself. Every figure must satisfy four requirements:

1. **Scientific scope is explicit.** The figure must say what problem it organizes and what stronger conclusion it does not license.
2. **Reader audit is possible.** The figure must link to a prose specification, assumptions, or a reproducible computational object.
3. **No clipped or overflowing text.** Labels must remain visibly inside their intended visual containers.
4. **No claim inflation.** A diagram of a proposed validation path is not described as an empirical validation result.

## Status boundary

The catalog is a publication and navigation record. The existence of a canonical figure does not imply that the corresponding empirical milestone has been completed. Prospective human validation, external validation, clinical validation, and adversarial theory tournaments remain future evidence milestones unless explicitly documented otherwise.
