# Literature Map

This is a curated starting map, not an exhaustive bibliography.

## Philosophical target

- Chalmers DJ. *Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness*. Journal of Consciousness Studies. 1995;2(3):200-219.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy. *Consciousness* and *The Neuroscience of Consciousness*.

## Global states and measurement concepts

- Bayne T, Hohwy J, Owen AM. *Are There Levels of Consciousness?* Trends in Cognitive Sciences. 2016;20(6):405-413. doi:10.1016/j.tics.2016.03.009.
- Kronemer SI, Bandettini PA, Gonzalez-Castillo J. *Sleuthing subjectivity: a review of covert measures of consciousness*. Nature Reviews Neuroscience. 2025;26:476-496. doi:10.1038/s41583-025-00934-1.

## Clinical disorders of consciousness

- Giacino JT et al. *Practice guideline update recommendations summary: Disorders of consciousness*. Neurology. 2018.
- Kondziella D et al. *European Academy of Neurology guideline on the diagnosis of coma and other disorders of consciousness*. European Journal of Neurology. 2020.
- Bodien YG et al. *Cognitive Motor Dissociation in Disorders of Consciousness*. New England Journal of Medicine. 2024;391:598-608. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa2400645.
- Wannez S et al. *The repetition of behavioral assessments in diagnosis of disorders of consciousness*. Annals of Neurology. 2017;81:883-889.

## Perturbational complexity

- Casali AG et al. *A theoretically based index of consciousness independent of sensory processing and behavior*. Science Translational Medicine. 2013;5(198):198ra105.
- Casarotto S et al. validation work on PCI in benchmark conscious/unconscious states and disorders of consciousness.

## Theory discrimination

- Melloni L et al. *Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness*. Nature. 2025;642:133-142. doi:10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1.

## Structural consciousness science

- Kleiner J. *Towards a structural turn in consciousness science*. Consciousness and Cognition. 2024;119:103653. doi:10.1016/j.concog.2024.103653.
- Lee-Youngzie Z, Tsuchiya N, Robinson M, Dietz D, Monti MM. *Towards a Mathematical Structure of Global Phenomenal Consciousness*. Entropy. 2026;28(6):615. doi:10.3390/e28060615. This category- and sheaf-theoretic proposal is treated here as an active frontier for linking local and global phenomenal structure, not as settled measurement theory.

## Methodological principle

The repository prioritizes sources that distinguish:

- measurement from explanation;
- report from experience;
- correlation from causal mechanism;
- state from content;
- behavioral responsiveness from covert cognition;
- theory-derived markers from theory-neutral benchmarks.
