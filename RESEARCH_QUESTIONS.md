# Research Questions

## Primary question

How can consciousness be measured scientifically when the experiential target is private and third-person science observes only evidence channels correlated with, caused by, enabling, or downstream from experience?

## Q1 - Measurement ontology
What exactly is the target: presence, global state, content, structure, capacity, or access/reportability?

## Q2 - Identifiability
Under what assumptions can a latent experiential target be identified or bounded from reports, behavior, neural data, perturbations, and physiological signals?

## Q3 - Necessary conditions
Which biological or dynamical conditions can be removed while preserving experience, and which removals reliably abolish it?

## Q4 - Sufficient conditions
Can any candidate physical/organizational condition be shown sufficient for experience rather than merely predictive in a familiar biological system?

## Q5 - Causal architecture
Which effective-connectivity, recurrent, thalamocortical, broadcast, integration, or complexity properties change causally with experience?

## Q6 - Phenomenal structure
Can the relational geometry/topology of experience be measured reproducibly and mapped to physical/neural structure?

## Q7 - Cross-state invariance
Which measurements transport across waking, sleep, dreaming, anesthesia, dissociative states, severe brain injury, and other altered states?

## Q8 - Theory discrimination
What preregistered observations would favor GNWT, IIT, recurrent processing, higher-order, structural, or dynamical accounts over rivals?

## Q9 - Negative evidence
When does a negative test meaningfully lower the probability of preserved experience, and when is it simply an insensitive assay?

## Q10 - Edge cases
How should evidence standards change for infants, nonhuman animals, organoids, and artificial systems where human-style report is unavailable?

## Q11 - Fabric
Does “fabric of consciousness” refer to a physical substrate, a causal organization, a dynamical regime, an informational/structural organization, a fundamental property, or a bridge law between physical and phenomenal structures? What unique predictions follow from each interpretation?

## Q12 - Limits
Which questions remain scientifically undecidable from third-person data without additional bridge principles?
