# Research Roadmap

This roadmap distinguishes completed infrastructure from future empirical milestones. A documentation artifact being complete does not mean the corresponding scientific hypothesis has been validated.

## Stage 0. Measurement contract

**Purpose:** define what counts as a valid measurement claim before data analysis.

Deliverables:

- target taxonomy: presence, global state, content, structure, capacity;
- M0-M7 claim ladder;
- Consciousness Evidence Profile (CEP) specification and schema;
- machine-readable claim schema;
- assumption registry;
- explicit inconclusive and abstention state;
- repository-wide publication policy.

Exit criteria:

- every empirical claim can name a target, evidence level, assumptions, validation domain, falsification condition, and nonclaims;
- negative evidence cannot be interpreted as absence when channel sensitivity is not established.

**Current status:** implemented as research infrastructure.

## Stage 1. Reproducible foundation

**Purpose:** establish auditable computational and documentation infrastructure.

Deliverables:

- unit-tested evidence-fusion scaffold;
- unit-tested CEP data structures;
- unit-tested structural-alignment scaffold;
- schema validation in CI;
- complete documentation map and glossary;
- reproducibility and data-governance contracts;
- preregistration template.

Exit criteria:

- `make check` passes;
- all reader-facing links resolve;
- examples validate against schemas;
- scientific boundaries are synchronized across documentation and code.

**Current status:** implemented as repository infrastructure.

## Stage 2. Benchmark reanalysis

**Purpose:** test whether candidate markers behave as expected on existing open datasets before collecting new data.

Deliverables:

- curated benchmark dataset registry;
- reproducible retrieval scripts where licensing permits;
- waking, sleep, and anesthesia analyses;
- single-channel baselines;
- cross-dataset transport analysis;
- documented failures and confounds.

Exit criteria:

- frozen preprocessing for confirmatory analyses;
- participant-level holdout;
- calibration and uncertainty reported;
- at least one external dataset used for transport analysis.

**Current status:** empirical work not yet completed.

## Stage 3. Prospective Phase 1 benchmark

**Purpose:** prospectively dissociate experience-related evidence from report, movement, attention, memory, and arousal.

Deliverables:

- preregistered protocol;
- healthy waking perceptual conditions;
- report and matched no-report conditions;
- sleep with structured awakening reports;
- medically supervised altered-state conditions where approved;
- synchronized EEG, physiology, event timing, and report;
- optional MEG/fMRI/TMS-EEG substudies.

Primary endpoints:

- held-out content calibration;
- report-operation invariance;
- cross-state transport loss;
- multimodal incremental value;
- held-out phenomenal-neural geometry prediction.

Exit criteria:

- primary endpoints analyzed exactly as preregistered;
- all confirmatory outcomes, including nulls, reported;
- data-quality failures separated from negative evidence.

**Current status:** protocol specified, data collection not yet completed.

## Stage 4. Phenomenal geometry

**Purpose:** determine whether relational structure of experience can be measured reliably and predicted from physical structure.

Deliverables:

- structured similarity and ordering protocol;
- reliability estimates for phenomenal distance matrices;
- preregistered neural and phenomenal representations;
- alignment, distortion, and null-model analyses;
- held-out contents and participants;
- robustness to alternative distance definitions.

Exit criteria:

- reproducible phenomenal structure above reliability floor;
- preregistered structural prediction evaluated out of sample;
- null and simpler mappings compared fairly.

**Current status:** mathematical framework implemented, empirical validation pending.

## Stage 5. Multimodal latent inference

**Purpose:** determine whether multiple channels add calibrated information about a latent experiential target.

Deliverables:

- explicit dependence models;
- strong single-channel baselines;
- partial-identification analysis;
- sensitivity to prior assumptions and missingness;
- abstention region;
- uncertainty-aware CEP output.

Exit criteria:

- multimodal model improves proper scoring metrics prospectively;
- improvement survives held-out participants and sites;
- dependence assumptions are stress-tested;
- failure domain is documented.

**Current status:** conceptual and software scaffolds implemented, empirical validation pending.

## Stage 6. Causal validation

**Purpose:** distinguish correlates from necessary, enabling, sufficient, or downstream processes.

Deliverables:

- perturbational experiments;
- state-dependent interventions;
- temporal predictions;
- causal graphical models where appropriate;
- negative controls for nonspecific arousal or motor effects.

Exit criteria:

- predicted target changes occur under controlled perturbation;
- alternative pathways are tested;
- causal language is limited to the intervention scope.

**Current status:** experimental design stage.

## Stage 7. Clinical collaboration

**Purpose:** evaluate multimodal inference in disorders of consciousness without turning one test into a bedside oracle.

Deliverables:

- multicenter preregistration;
- serial standardized behavioral assessment;
- EEG and sleep/wake characterization;
- task EEG/fMRI where feasible;
- resting quantitative markers;
- perturbational measures in equipped centers;
- repeated assessments for fluctuation;
- external-site validation and abstention analysis.

Exit criteria:

- prospectively locked model;
- external-site calibration;
- explicit discordance analysis;
- failure-to-acquire and failure-to-interpret rates reported;
- regulatory and clinical boundaries maintained.

**Current status:** framework specified, clinical validation not yet completed.

## Stage 8. Adversarial theory tournament

**Purpose:** test risky predictions that discriminate candidate theories.

Deliverables:

- theory-specific proposition list;
- common stimuli and acquisition where possible;
- preregistered divergent predictions;
- pass/fail regions;
- independent or blinded confirmatory analysis;
- explicit auxiliary assumptions;
- full publication of theory-supporting and theory-challenging outcomes.

Exit criteria:

- at least one genuinely divergent prediction evaluated prospectively;
- post hoc reinterpretation separated from confirmatory inference;
- theory failure localized to the tested proposition and assumptions.

**Current status:** falsification framework implemented, tournament pending.

## Stage 9. Edge-case transfer

**Purpose:** test which measurement principles transfer beyond communicative adult humans.

Domains:

- infants;
- nonhuman animals;
- organoids and engineered neural systems;
- artificial systems.

Exit criteria:

- domain-specific validation rather than threshold copying;
- explicit substrate assumptions;
- separate scientific-evidence and ethical-precaution thresholds;
- no inference from behavioral fluency alone in AI.

**Current status:** evidence standards specified, empirical transfer studies pending.

## Repository-wide completion criteria

The program is not considered to have produced a general consciousness measure until a target-specific instrument demonstrates all of the following in a declared scope:

- prospective preregistration;
- calibration;
- participant-level and site-level external validation;
- cross-state robustness;
- confound resistance;
- explicit uncertainty;
- abstention for insufficient evidence;
- documented false-negative and false-positive pathways;
- transportability across acquisition systems where claimed;
- published failure domain;
- added value beyond strong single-channel baselines;
- claim language no stronger than the evidence supports.

A universal scalar requires additional evidence that the scalar preserves meaning across targets, states, populations, and relevant substrates. No such scalar is assumed at project start.
