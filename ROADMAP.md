# Research Roadmap

## Stage 0 - Measurement contract
- adopt the Consciousness Evidence Profile (CEP) schema;
- maintain the claim registry and M0-M7 claim ladder;
- preregister falsification conditions before confirmatory data access;
- preserve an explicit inconclusive/abstention state.

## Stage 1 - Foundation
- formalize target taxonomy;
- formalize measurement assumptions;
- build reproducible toy models;
- curate literature and benchmark datasets;
- define preregistration templates.

## Stage 2 - Benchmark analysis
- reanalyze open waking/sleep/anesthesia EEG datasets;
- compare complexity, spectral, connectivity, and perturbational proxies;
- quantify cross-state and cross-dataset transport.

## Stage 3 - Phenomenal geometry
- design report protocol for similarity judgments;
- validate reliability of phenomenal distance matrices;
- implement neural/phenomenal alignment metrics;
- preregister held-out predictions.

## Stage 4 - Multimodal fusion
- build latent-variable models with explicit dependence;
- compare against single-channel baselines;
- implement partial-identification and sensitivity analysis.

## Stage 5 - Causal validation
- integrate perturbation data;
- test necessary/enabling versus downstream signatures;
- distinguish neural prerequisites, correlates, and consequences.

## Stage 6 - Clinical collaboration
- preregister multicenter DoC protocol;
- harmonize CRS-R, EEG, fMRI, TMS-EEG, and physiology;
- external-site validation and abstention analysis.

## Stage 7 - Theory tournament
- adversarially preregister unique predictions across candidate theories;
- blind confirmatory analyses;
- publish pass/fail outcomes and unresolved ambiguities.

## Stage 8 - Edge cases
- define evidence transfer criteria for infants, animals, organoids, and AI;
- avoid assuming human neural signatures are substrate-universal;
- develop ethically conservative uncertainty reporting.

## Repository-wide completion criteria
The program is not considered to have produced a general consciousness measure until a target-specific instrument demonstrates prospective calibration, external-site transport, cross-state robustness, confound resistance, explicit uncertainty, and a documented failure domain. A universal scalar requires additional evidence that the scalar preserves meaning across targets and populations; no such scalar is assumed at project start.
